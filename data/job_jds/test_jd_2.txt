SOC Analyst I — Managed Security Services

About the role:
Join our 24/7 security operations center supporting mid-market clients across
finance and healthcare. You will be the first line of triage on security alerts
and will work alongside senior analysts who handle escalation and threat hunting.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor SIEM alert queues and triage according to documented runbooks
- Investigate phishing reports submitted by client end users
- Perform initial packet and log analysis to confirm or dismiss alerts
- Escalate confirmed incidents to Tier 2 with a written summary
- Maintain case notes and contribute to runbook improvements

Requirements:
- Linux and Windows operating system fundamentals
- Network protocol analysis using tcpdump or Wireshark
- Scripting ability in Python or Bash for routine automation
- Familiarity with the MITRE ATT&CK framework
- Clear written communication for incident documentation

Nice to have:
- Exposure to digital forensics tooling
- Coursework or labs in vulnerability assessment
- Security+ or equivalent certification

New graduates are welcome to apply. We provide structured onboarding and
mentorship for the first six months.

Schedule: Rotating shifts including some nights and weekends.
Location: Hybrid, three days onsite.
